Questions

Do you need my password?

No. Your store platform lets you approve a "collaborator" with access to only the parts of your admin the job needs. You can remove it the moment the job is done.

Why no phone calls?

Email means you get everything in writing: what's wrong, the price, and what was changed. You can reply whenever suits you.

What if you can't fix it?

Then you don't pay. If I find it's something only your platform's support or an app developer can fix, I'll tell you exactly who to contact and what to say.

How fast is it?

Most quick fixes are done within a day or two of you approving the price.

Will you change things I didn't ask for?

No. I only touch what we agreed on, and anything else I notice goes in the note for you to decide.
